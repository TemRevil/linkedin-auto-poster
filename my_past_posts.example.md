## Style-picking heuristics (for Claude when generating)
- News/industry topic → Style A
- "What I built / learned" → Style B
- Quick reaction / hot take → Style C
- "How to / how it works" → Style D

## Universal rules (apply to ALL styles)
- Sound human. No corporate jargon, no LinkedIn-bro phrases.
- Mobile-first formatting: blank lines between thoughts.
- 3-5 hashtags max. 0-2 emojis.
