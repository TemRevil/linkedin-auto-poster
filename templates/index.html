Documentation

How this app works and how to set it up

What this app does

It scrapes AI/tech news daily, lets you swipe topics you like, then has Claude write LinkedIn posts in your voice. You review, edit, approve — and it posts to LinkedIn for you via browser automation.

1 · Set up your profile

Run the setup wizard (button above, or Setup & help in the sidebar). It writes your profile.md — your name, role, audience, and voice. Claude reads this every time it writes.

2 · Connect LinkedIn

Go to Settings → Accounts and click Login. A browser opens — sign in once and the session is saved. You can keep up to 3 accounts.

3 · Tune your voice

Under Settings → Voice, paste 3-5 of your best real posts into the A/B/C/D styles. The closer these match how you actually write, the better the output.

4 · Generate a post

On Home, type what you want in the chat bar — e.g. "hot take on MCP protocol" or "builder update about my side project". Mention Gmail / newsletter to pull from your inbox (if connected). Watch the live progress as Claude works.

5 · Review & approve

Open Posts → Pending. Edit inline (auto-saves), attach images (multiple = carousel), rewrite shorter/longer, then Approve to publish. Don't like it? Reject with a reason — Claude learns from it.

Discover & Network

Discover shows news cards — swipe right to like (trains your topic taste), left to skip. Network scrapes and analyzes your LinkedIn connections so posts target the right audience.

If a post fails

Check Posts → Failed. Each card shows the exact error and an error screenshot, plus a Retry button. You can attach images there too before retrying.

Your privacy

Everything personal stays local: profile, past posts, connections, LinkedIn sessions, API keys, and generated posts are all gitignored. Nothing leaves your machine except the posts you approve.

CLI commands (optional)

$ python run.py approve # Open this dashboard

Why are you rejecting this post?

Your feedback teaches Claude what NOT to do next time. Be specific. e.g. "too generic, no personal angle", "starts with the article title", "feels like marketing copy".
